# Using the monthly budget

A **monthly budget** is one branch's plan for one month: the income it expects
to take, and what it means to spend on each expense category. You write it
before the month is spent, and the screen sets it beside what actually
happened.

Only an admin can see or write budgets. Branch staff never see this screen.

The plan is the only thing saved. The real figures next to it are added up from
the payments and expenses as they stand, every time the page opens — so a
payment or an expense recorded late shows up in the comparison the moment it
goes in, even for a month that's already over.

## Finding the screen

In the sidebar, under **Money**, choose **Monthly budget**. It opens on the
current month. The dashboard also has an **Against the plan** link that brings
you here on the month you were looking at.

Change the month in the **Month** box and press **Show**. The month travels in
the address, so a particular month can be bookmarked or sent to somebody.

## Every branch at a glance

The first screen is every branch, side by side, for the month you picked.

Four cards across the top, for the whole college: **income planned**, **income
actual**, **expenses actual**, and the **net balance** with what you'd planned
for underneath it.

Then one row per branch: income planned, income actual, expenses planned,
expenses actual, and the net balance. A branch that hasn't been planned for
says **No plan yet** under its name — its actual figures still show, because
they're real money either way. The last row adds every branch up as **The whole
college**.

A net balance in red means that branch spent more than it took that month.

Click a branch name to open its plan.

## One branch's month

The branch screen has three parts: the headline figures, the line-by-line
comparison, and the form that writes the plan.

At the top, four cards — **income**, **expenses**, the **net balance**, and
**against the plan**, which is how the month came out compared with what you
expected. The first three each carry what was planned underneath.

The heading tells you who wrote the plan, or that there isn't one yet. Use
**Every branch** at the top left to go back.

## Writing or changing a plan

Plans are written on a branch's own page, not on the first screen. Go to
**Monthly budget**, set the month, click the **branch name** in the table, and
scroll to the bottom: the card titled **The plan for Main Branch** (or whatever
the branch is called) is where a plan is written and rewritten.

| Field | What to put in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Income you expect (USD)** | What you think the branch will take that month, from every source. Required |
| **One box per category** | What you plan to spend on Rent, Electricity and the rest. Leave a box empty for anything you're not planning for |
| **Note** | Anything worth remembering about this month, like "Rent goes up from the 15th" |

Press **Save the plan**. The message names the branch and the month:
*"Main Branch's plan for Sept 2026 saved."*

Things worth knowing:

- **A branch has one plan per month.** Saving again replaces what was there
  rather than adding a second plan, so the form is also how you edit.
- **An empty box isn't the same as a zero you have to justify.** It simply
  means nothing was planned for that category. If money goes out on it anyway,
  the comparison still shows it — see **Not planned** below.
- **Clearing a box removes that line.** The plan is rewritten wholesale each
  time you save, so emptying Stationery and saving takes Stationery out of the
  plan.
- **Only active categories get a box**, plus any deactivated one this plan
  already has an amount for — so saving again doesn't quietly drop that line.
- **Nothing you do here touches real money.** A plan is a plan; the payments
  and expenses are untouched.

## Removing a plan

On the same branch page, **Remove the plan** sits at the top right, next to the
heading. It appears only once that branch has a plan for that month, and it
throws the plan away after a confirmation. Only the plan goes: every payment and expense recorded for that
month stays exactly as it is, and the branch simply reads **No plan yet**
again.

## Reading the comparison

The **Planned against actual** table runs: income first, then one row per
expense category, then total expenses, then the net balance. Every row shows
what was planned, what actually happened, and the difference.

The difference column is written to be read at a glance:

- **On plan** when the two match exactly.
- A **+** or **−** amount otherwise.
- **Red when it's the wrong way round** — taking *less* income than planned,
  spending *more* than budgeted, or ending the month *below* the net balance
  you expected. A difference in your favour is left plain.
- **Not planned** in the planned column for a category you didn't budget for.
  Anything spent on it still appears in the actual column, so unplanned
  spending can't hide.

A few things about the actual figures:

- **They're in dollars, like the plan.** Money taken or spent in shillings is
  counted at today's exchange rate, so the figures are the same "Combined at
  today's rate" figures the dashboard shows. They say what the month's money
  is worth now, so a past month's result moves when the rate changes.
- **Income** is every payment taken at that branch that month — registration
  fees, monthly fees, books, examination fees and anything else — counted by
  the day the money came in.
- **Expenses** are every expense booked to that branch that month, counted by
  the day the money went out. Teacher salaries are in there like any other
  cost.
- **The net balance** is one minus the other: money in minus money out, over
  that month. Negative, shown in red, means the branch spent more than it
  took. It isn't profit — nothing is counted until the money actually moves,
  so fees a student still owes don't lift it, and what a percentage teacher
  has earned doesn't lower it until the day you pay them.
- A **deactivated category** keeps its row whenever this month planned or spent
  something on it, so old months still add up.

Your note, if you wrote one, sits under the table.

## When the system says no

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *"Enter the income you expect."* | The income box can't be left empty. Put your best estimate in |
| *"Enter an amount like 300 or 300.50."* | One of the category boxes has something that isn't an amount in it. The box is marked |
| *"Pick the month this plan is for."* | Reload the page and try again — the month didn't come through |
| *"That branch no longer exists."* | The branch was deleted while you had the page open |
| *"There's no plan for that month to remove."* | Somebody removed it already. Reload to see where things stand |
| *"No branches yet. Add one before planning a month."* | Set up a branch first, under Admin → Branches |

## Everyday tasks

**Plan next month for a branch.** Monthly budget → set the month → click the
branch → fill in the income you expect and an amount for each category you plan
to spend on → **Save the plan**.

**See how last month went.** Monthly budget → set the month → read the branch
rows, or click a branch for the line-by-line comparison.

**Find out where a branch overspent.** Open the branch for that month and look
down the difference column for the red figures.

**Copy last month's plan into this month.** Open last month's plan, note the
amounts, switch the month at the top, and type them into the new form. There's
no copy button yet.

**Change a plan mid-month.** Open the branch and save the form again with the
new amounts. The old plan is replaced.

**Start over.** **Remove the plan**, then write a new one. Your payments and
expenses aren't affected.
