# One ledger for every payment

Every dollar the college takes in is one row in `payments`: a registration fee, a month of a skill, a book sold at the counter. ADR-0003 kept registration fees on the enrollment because there was only one kind of payment and no way to know what the rest would need; now that monthly fees, payment methods and daily income are here, that reason is gone, and the paid registration fees have moved into the ledger as the earlier decision said they would. The enrollment still holds the fee it owes, because that's a price the admin can waive, but whether it was paid is the ledger's answer alone, so there is one place that says what the college was paid and one place a day's income is counted from.

A month is paid in full or not at all, the same as a registration fee, and the database refuses a second registration fee for an enrollment or a second payment for a fee month. Nothing stores a running total: a day's takings, a category's income and a teacher's earnings are all counted from the payments every time they're asked for. Taking a payment back is a delete, not a reversing entry — this is a small college's books, an admin does it only for money recorded by mistake, and a ledger nobody can correct is worse than one that can be. Part payments would need a different shape and are deliberately not supported.
